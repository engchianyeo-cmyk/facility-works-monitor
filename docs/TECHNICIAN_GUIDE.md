# Technician Guide

Technicians accept and execute directly assigned work and authorized team incidents. They cannot act on another Technician’s assignment by default.

## Work procedure

1. Sign in with your own account and review location, scope, priority, safety context, and evidence requirements.
2. **Accept** before starting; select **Start** when execution begins.
3. Record accurate notes, labour, evidence, parts, and exceptions.
4. Select **Complete** only when defined work is finished and safe for review.
5. Respond to clarification/rework without altering historical evidence.

Do not share credentials, fabricate timestamps, expose sensitive data, or close/cancel records outside your authority.

## Emergency procedure

Open the stable authenticated incident link. If assigned, acknowledge immediately and record Mobilising, On Site, Rescue In Progress, Safe, and Recovery at their actual times. Declare **Safe** only when immediate danger is controlled under approved procedures. Request linked corrective work where repair remains.

Notification failure does not invalidate the incident. Maintain permitted mobile/WhatsApp details and never place credentials or tokens in messages.
