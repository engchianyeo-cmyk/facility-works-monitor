## Required pre-read

- Completed [release gate](RELEASE_1_2_CHECKLIST.md)
- Signed [UAT record](UAT_SIGNOFF.md)
- Release notes and [known issues](KNOWN_ISSUES.md)
- Migration, backup and fingerprint evidence
- [Deployment](DEPLOYMENT_RUNBOOK.md) and [rollback](ROLLBACK_PLAN.md) plans
- Monitoring, support and communications plan

## Agenda

1. Confirm candidate identity and scope freeze.
2. Review tests, security, migration, Preview and UAT evidence.
3. Review risks, outstanding defects and known limitations.
4. Confirm backup, recovery point, rollback/forward-fix decision and owners.
5. Confirm deployment window, operator, support and communications.
6. Poll authorities and record the decision.
