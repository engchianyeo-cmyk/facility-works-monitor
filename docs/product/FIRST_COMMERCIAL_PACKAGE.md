# FMWorks First Commercial Package

## Package decision

Sell a **paid, fixed-scope Engineering Operations Control pilot**, not an enterprise EAM replacement and not an open-ended software subscription.

Working name: **FMWorks Operations Control – Single Site Pilot**

The package is for facilities teams currently coordinating operational issues through spreadsheets, email and messaging, especially where incident response, work authorization and proof of completion are fragmented.

## Ideal first customer

- One operational site or a tightly bounded building portfolio.
- 10–50 named operational users across facilities management, supervision, technical execution and approval.
- A visible coordination problem with reactive work and incidents.
- A sponsor able to define authority, nominate process owners and supply usable facility data.
- No requirement that FMWorks replace ERP, procurement, BIM, GIS or an established global EAM in the pilot.

Avoid first customers whose buying decision depends on full PM, asset, inventory, offline, commissioning, multi-tenant or enterprise-integration capability before those modules are production proven.

## Packaging roadmap

| Horizon | Offer | Included / excluded |
|---|---|---|
| **SELL NOW** | Single Site Operations Control Pilot | Authenticated roles, controlled work-order lifecycle, assignments, operational dashboard/workspace, audit trail, incident coordination where deployment is verified, sample/customer-provided facility view, onboarding and outcome review |
| **NEXT PAID** | Technician Evidence & Operational Readiness add-on | Reviewed completion-evidence workflow, field-optimized completion, evidence access controls, blockers/dependencies, readiness model, notifications with provider-confirmed delivery state |
| **FUTURE** | Maintenance Foundation; Commissioning & Handover | Asset register, PM, parts integration; later test packs, witness/hold points, punch/retest, concessions, dossier and readiness certification |
| **PARTNER** | Enterprise connections | SSO/identity, ERP/procurement, BIM/CAD/GIS, IoT, messaging, BI, e-signature, specialist reliability analytics |
| **DO NOT BUILD** | Commodity platforms outside the wedge | General ledger, supplier payment network, CAD/BIM authoring, GIS engine, generic HR/timekeeping, broad low-code platform, ungoverned general chatbot |

“Sell now” is conditional on deployment verification, security review, authentic browser product review and a buyer-specific scope statement. It is not a claim that every locally documented module is already live.

## Pilot scope

### Included outcomes

1. One controlled channel for operational work and incident records.
2. Role-appropriate authorization, assignment and action.
3. A Mission Control view of critical, overdue and blocked operational work.
4. Persistent activity history for material workflow actions.
5. A customer-configured site/building/zone vocabulary and facility layout source when supplied.
6. Pilot configuration, data-loading assistance, role training and weekly outcome review.
7. Export/handback plan agreed before start.

### Explicit exclusions

- Full enterprise asset management, PM generation or spare-parts accounting.
- Accounting, invoicing, purchase orders or vendor payments.
- Offline mobile guarantee.
- BIM/GIS authoring or a digital twin.
- Autonomous AI decisions.
- Formal commissioning certification unless contracted after the module is built and accepted.
- Guaranteed external notification delivery without a configured provider and delivery receipt.

## Acceptance gates before contracting

- Product review completed using an authentic browser and target viewports.
- Production environment and applied migration set reconciled with source control.
- Role/permission matrix approved by the customer.
- Data classification, retention, backup and support responsibilities documented.
- Incident and work-order happy/degraded paths demonstrated.
- No demo feature is described as live unless the backing schema and provider are deployed.
- Pilot success baseline and measurement method agreed.

## Proposed delivery model

| Phase | Indicative duration | Deliverable |
|---|---:|---|
| Discovery and control design | 1 week | scoped facility, roles, workflow, priorities, outcomes and exclusions |
| Configuration and data preparation | 1–2 weeks | users, departments, facility vocabulary, sample/customer data, role tests |
| Guided rollout | 2 weeks | trained cohort, supervised live use, issue resolution |
| Measured pilot | 4–6 weeks | weekly metrics and decision review |
| Value review | 1 week | outcome report, risks, go/stop/expand decision and next-scope proposal |

These durations are proposals, not commitments. Integration, migration, customer security review and data quality can change the plan.

## Success measures

Select three to five buyer-owned measures rather than promising generic ROI:

- median time from request to accountable owner;
- median time from critical incident declaration to first recorded action;
- percentage of material transitions with complete actor/time/history;
- overdue high-priority work exposure;
- number and age of blocked records without an owner;
- management time spent reconciling spreadsheets/messages;
- user ability to identify the top operational issue within 30 seconds;
- technician task completion or blocker-reporting time.

Do not attribute avoided downtime or savings to FMWorks without an agreed baseline and customer validation.

## Commercial model

Use a fixed pilot fee with a defined user/site envelope, implementation effort and success review. Quote subscription pricing only after hosting, support, data retention, service levels, tenant model and ongoing operating costs are approved.

Commercial proposal should separate:

- software access;
- configuration/onboarding services;
- customer-specific integrations or data migration;
- third-party provider costs;
- enhanced support or service levels;
- future module delivery.

Avoid copying per-technician pricing mechanically. Unlimited or low-cost requester access reduces friction, but FMWorks needs an economically tested model that reflects sites, operational users, modules and implementation support.

## Buyer proof pack

Before the first paid conversation advances to contract, provide:

1. authentic 10-minute role-based demo;
2. architecture and data-boundary summary;
3. security and permission overview;
4. current versus planned capability statement;
5. implementation plan and named responsibilities;
6. support, backup, incident and recovery commitments;
7. data import/export and termination approach;
8. pilot success measures;
9. referenceable test/build evidence;
10. pricing, assumptions and exclusions.

## Commercial objections

| Objection | Honest response |
|---|---|
| “Why not Maximo or SAP?” | Use them when deep global EAM/ERP scope is required. FMWorks is a focused operational decision layer with faster, narrower adoption goals. |
| “Why not MaintainX, Limble or UpKeep?” | Choose them for mature general CMMS breadth today. Choose the FMWorks pilot to prove exception-led engineering operations, incident linkage and readiness clarity. |
| “Does it replace our BIM/GIS?” | No. It should consume governed drawings/locations and add operational overlays. |
| “Is the AI making decisions?” | No production autonomous decision is claimed. Future assistance remains evidence-linked, permission-aware and human-confirmed. |
| “Is commissioning included?” | Not in the first package. It is a differentiated future paid module after implementation and acceptance. |
| “Is every local module live?” | No. Deployment state is verified explicitly; local or migration-pending capability is not represented as production functionality. |

## Go / no-go rule

Proceed with a paid pilot only if the buyer values the current wedge, accepts the explicit exclusions and can supply a bounded operational use case. Do not close a deal by promising roadmap modules as delivered product.
