# Supervisor Guide

Supervisors coordinate workload, assignments, operational teams, permitted department activities, emergency rosters, and exceptions. They do not receive Administrator identity-management authority.

## Daily cycle

1. Review open, overdue, due-today, and pending-approval work.
2. Confirm approved work has an appropriate active Technician, vendor, or team.
3. Monitor acceptance, start, completion, and evidence quality.
4. Resolve reassignment/capacity conflicts without erasing history.
5. Escalate identity, security, configuration, or commercial issues to the correct owner.

Use one primary assignment and confirm skill, availability, location, and safety requirements. Never use shared Technician accounts.

## Emergency response

Maintain roster entries and effective periods. Ensure Administrators, Supervisors, and responders have current contact details. An unassigned incident requires explicit coordination; notification does not equal acknowledgement. Follow the approved manual escalation procedure until a scheduler exists.

Before closure, check notes, labour, evidence, residual hazards, follow-up work, and review. A situation being safe is not the same as corrective repair being complete.
