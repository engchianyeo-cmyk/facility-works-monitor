# WP-WO-009 — Completed Work, Evidence, Contractor and Commercial Controls

## Approved operating rules

1. `Completed` is the Work Order state. Technicians use **Mark as Completed**.
2. The assigned Technician-in-Charge may mark work Completed from Assigned or In Progress when physical work has already been performed. A direct Assigned → Completed transition is audited.
3. **After evidence is mandatory** before a Work Order can be marked Completed. Before evidence is expected whenever the original fault/site condition can safely be captured.
4. Only **Supervisor, Facility Manager or Administrator** may accept Completed Work or return it for rework. The person who submitted Completed Work cannot accept their own work.
5. Technician assignment is manual by authorised operational management. Fictitious/default Technician B assignment is not an accepted operating rule.
6. Contractor/service-provider master data supports nominated contractors, emergency capability and service categories.
7. Pre-agreed contractor rate items support labour, material, equipment, service and call-out unit rates with effective dates.
8. Emergency work follows **fix first, costing later** where necessary. Costing remains auditable and requires subsequent approval.
9. External contractor payment assessment is created after Completed Work is accepted. Default payment terms are 30 days.
10. Facility area codes support precise fault location and future drawing/map linkage.

## Initial contractor service categories

Lift Maintenance; HVAC; Fire Alarm Testing; Fire Protection System; Plumbing and Sanitary; Electrical - Transformer; Electrical - Switchgear; Electrical - General; Solar Power System; Cleaning Systems; Fumigation Service; Landscaping; Building Fit-out / Interior Finishes; Lighting Control System; Building Management System; Pumps / Rotating Equipment; Generator / UPS; Other.

## Authorised liaison disciplines

Two governed liaison disciplines are provided for pre-agreed proposals and rates:

- Building fit-out / interior finishes / landscaping
- Electrical and mechanical services

## Costing chain

Contractor rate schedule → workers/material/equipment actually used → Technician-in-Charge certification → Supervisor/Facility Manager/Administrator verification → payment assessment → Finance approval/payment.

## Emergency chain

Fault/incident → precise area/asset → authorised assignment → nominated contractor/TIC dispatch → physical work → Before/After evidence → Mark as Completed → independent verification → costing regularisation → payment assessment → Close.
